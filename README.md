# apotek-alpro-dashboard

## BOD Dashboard (Chief only)

Board-level outlet performance view, inside the Executive Dashboard as the **BOD Dashboard** tab (also `bod-dashboard.html`).

- **Data**: read live from the Google Sheet (`MASTER` sheet in full, `Exec Dashboard` rows 9-25) through the sheet's gviz endpoint. No data is stored in this repo.
- **Files**: `bod-engine.js` (parsing, periods, levels, PAT), `bod-core.js` (access gate, filters, drawers), `bod-tabs-outlets.js`, `bod-tabs-insights.js`.
- **Access**: Chief always has the tab. Any other user gets it only when **BOD Dashboard** is ticked on that user (Admin Config > edit user > Department Access Permissions, saved in the user's `department_access` as `bod`; no SQL needed). It is not part of any role's default list, so ticking it for one person never opens it for their whole role. Enforced in `getAccessibleTabs`, `showTab` and inside the page itself.
- **Definitions**: growth = running year-on-year (same months, 12 months earlier). Levels 5..1 = 20 / 15 / 10 / 5 / 0 percent, on sales or transactions. PAT = PSH x working hours per outlet-month (no other cost deducted). Filters: year, period, revenue/transactions, outlet type, area manager, province and Kota (the Kota list follows the selected province). Outlet detail drawer (click any outlet row, bod-drawer.js): its own period filter (year, period, month; 'Dashboard period' resets it), narrative (opening, first sales, age, last sales, status), a 'flags to pay attention to' panel at the top (the same 10 rules, thresholds and priority score as the Watchlist tab, re-evaluated on the drawer's own period, each flag with its evidence, e.g. 'Transactions -13.2% vs FY2025', 'PAT Aug/Sep/Oct negative', and its rule), cards (sales with matched-month YoY, estimated PAT, weighted PSH with rank, latest-month PAT, status), a monthly trend with a measure selector (revenue, transactions, basket, margin, profit, PSH, PAT, hours, staff sales/day, focus, member, recruitment, new members, salary/rent/utilities/stock loss %) against the prior-year months, an 'All parameters' table (current vs prior-year period, change, month coverage), outlet economics (per-month cost lines, PAT margin, per-m2 rows once Size is filled), a peer comparison (median of same-type outlets of the same maturity, needs 8+ peers, rank per measure), and a 24-month table, charts and CSV. PSH ranking tab: every trading outlet ranked from highest to lowest PSH for the selected period and filters (period PSH = PAT / working hours over the period's months), with prior-year PSH, change, rank move vs the prior-year ranking, latest-month PSH, sales, margin, PAT, quartile bands, loss-making flag, a top/bottom 10 chart, a grouped ranking (area manager, outlet type, Kota, province), search and CSV export; toggle to exclude new outlets and set the minimum months of PSH data. Profitability (PAT) tab (bod-tabs-pat.js): company, outlet and group (area manager, outlet type, Kota, province) profitability over a selectable look-back window ending at the dashboard period (last 3 months, last 6 months, last 12 months, or the dashboard period), each compared with the window just before it and with the same months a year earlier. A linear trend over the last 4/6/9/12 months of PAT gives a trend line, a 3-month forecast (with a +/-1 s.d. band), the months to breakeven for loss-making outlets, and a 'hold flat' scenario (average of the last 3 months) beside it; the forecast is indicative only (straight line, no seasonality or openings). Outlets are classed by journey: profitable and growing, profitable but slipping, turning (loss but recent months positive or breakeven within 6 months), narrowing loss, deepening loss, or too little data. Toggle to mature outlets only, search, CSV export. Growth vs profitability tab (bod-tabs-quad.js): a quadrant bubble chart of every comparable mature outlet, x = like-for-like growth of the dashboard period (sales or transactions, as chosen in the top bar), y = estimated PAT margin (PAT / sales) over the same period, bubble size = sales, giving four quadrants (profitable and growing, profitable and declining, loss-making and growing, loss-making and declining). Growth can be split at 0% or at the company like-for-like growth. New outlets and outlets without a full prior-year base have no growth rate, so they are counted under 'Not plotted' and listed in the table; axes follow the 2nd-98th percentile with outliers pinned to the edge. Click a bubble or row for the outlet drawer, click a quadrant card to filter the table, CSV export. Geographic analysis tab: zones by Kecamatan or Kota compared on growth, sales per mature outlet per day, margin, PSH and PAT, with a signal per zone (plateau = expansion candidate, lagging, winning, growing, stalled = fix first, under-penetrated) judged relative to the company's own like-for-like growth; no coordinates in the sheet, so a 1-2 km radius is a shortlist to verify on a map. New outlet = within 6 months of opening (opening month = month 1, counted to the period's last month); older outlets lacking a full prior-year base are shown separately as 'not yet comparable'. Financial year = Jan-Dec (FY2026 = Jan 2026 to the latest month, year to date; rolls over each January). FY2025 = Jan-Dec 2025; FY2024 = Jul-Dec 2024 only (the workbook's reliable history starts Jul 2024), so growth for FY2025 starts from Jul 2025 (use Half-year 2) and every period is comparable from FY2026. Running 12 months = last 12 months to the latest month (e.g. Nov 2025 - Oct 2026), moving forward automatically. Company-level PSH uses the Exec Dashboard figure when no outlet filter is applied; outlet and filtered views use outlet PSH x hours.
- **Dynamic sheet**: when a new month is added (every metric block gains a column and everything shifts right), nothing needs changing. MASTER months and metric blocks are found by header text, the fixed info columns (Outlet, AM, Type, ...) by their header names, and the Exec Dashboard reads however many month columns have data. The latest month, FY selector and default period follow the sheet.
- **Navigation (two levels)**: 13 former tabs are now 4 groups + Overview, with a sub-view bar and a scope strip (period, outlet count, removable filter chips, Reset filters). **Overview**; **Outlet movement** = Like-for-like movers (Declining / Improving switch, replaces two tabs), New outlets, Watchlist & alerts; **Financial performance** = Growth scorecard, Profitability (PAT), PSH ranking, Growth vs profit, Winning formula; **Segments & geography** = Area managers, Geographic, Store size. Every view keeps its own content; nothing was dropped. Old view ids still work for cross-links (`S.tab='watch'`, overview alert tiles, AM row click). Each view's long explanation is a collapsed 'About this view' row (state remembered). Badges: declining/improving counts on Like-for-like movers, new outlets, high alerts on Watchlist. Views not merged on purpose: PAT, PSH ranking and Growth vs profit answer different questions with different table shapes; Geographic and Store size use different grouping logic.
- **Data check (audit)**: Data notes now has a month-by-month table of MASTER rebuilt vs the Exec Dashboard sales and profit rows. Known source differences: Exec profit differs from the MASTER profit sum in most months, and older Exec sales include closed outlets that are no longer in MASTER, so MASTER-based total (non-LFL) growth can read 2-4 pp above the Exec Dashboard growth. The Growth scorecard shows a reference row 'Sales per Exec Dashboard' when no filter is set. Like-for-like, PAT and size figures are not affected (independently rebuilt from the raw sheet with 0 mismatches).
- **Tabs (views)**: Overview, Declining stores, Improving stores, New outlets, Financial growth (group by opening year, type, tier, store size, AM, province, nature, city), Area managers scorecard, Store size & profitability, Watchlist & alerts, Winning formula.
- Targets are intentionally not used.
- **Store size & profitability** tab reads MASTER `Size Category` (SS/S/M/L/XL) and `Size Sales Floor (m2)` (header-matched, so columns can move). It shows size-band economics (floor-weighted sales per m2, PAT per m2, rent per m2, staff hours per m2, PAT margin, PSH, like-for-like growth, share of profitable outlets), each outlet's sales per m2 against the median of its own size category (above / in line / below at +/-20%, categories need 5 outlets), a floor-size scatter, and CSV export. Outlets with a blank size are shown as `Not recorded` and left out of per-m2 figures (nothing estimated). Size is also a top-bar filter (`Store size`), a group dimension in Financial growth, Profitability (PAT) and PSH ranking, and the outlet drawer shows size pill, per-m2 economics and a size-category comparison. Header fix: `tier` now reads `Category 2026` (previously it picked up `Size Category`).
